# Player Guide

How to play Clan Chaos, for you and your friends. The exact numbers are in `rules.md`.

## Goal
Finish with the most **victory points** after 8 rounds. Points come from successful raids (+2), destroying forts (+3), eliminating rivals (+4), event bonuses, an intact fort at the end (+2) and leftover resources (+1 per 5 of each kind).

## Getting started
1. One person opens the game and taps **Create Room**, then shares the 6-character code (or the link).
2. Everyone else taps **Join** and types the code.
3. Pick a clan, tap **I'm ready**. The host taps **Start match** (needs 2–8 players, all ready).

## A round in 75 seconds
| Phase | Time | What you do |
|---|---|---|
| **Build & Gather** | 30 s | Send parties to gather, buy cards, recruit mercenaries, build barricades, repair, trade, chat, scout |
| **Scheme & Commit** | 30 s | Same as above, plus **commit one secret raid** and any defense cards. Your orders are locked when the timer ends |
| **Resolution** | 15 s | All raids are revealed and resolved one after another. Watch the reveal, check your losses |

Gathering and building also work during Scheme. Once Resolution starts, orders are locked.

## Your screen
- **Top (always visible):** round, phase, what the phase is for, the countdown, and the next deadline. Tap **What can I do?** for the full list.
- **Next:** your most important next step, then your gold 🪙, materials 🪵, food 🍖 (each out of 100), fort 🏰, mercenaries, points and clan ability. Tap any of them for an explanation.
- **Map:** tap a site to send gathering parties, tap a stronghold to scout, trade or raid. 🛡 = safe site, ⚔ = contested. The label shows stock (Rich / Steady / Low). Your parties are listed under the map. Open **Map legend** for every symbol.
- **Panels:** Actions · Cards · Raid · Feed · Players · Trade · Chat (bottom bar on phones).

## Doing things
- **Gather:** tap a site and press **Send here**, or tap a party under the map and choose a destination. It walks there, works for 8–15 seconds, and walks home with its load (up to 6). You can **Recall** a party early and keep part of its load.
- **Raid:** open **Raid** (you can draft during Build), pick a target (a stronghold or an ambush at a contested site), optionally attach cards, choose what to steal first, check the attack vs defense preview, then **Review & submit** during Scheme. The status goes Draft → Submitted → Locked → Resolved. You get one raid per round. Rivals can see *that* you submitted, not what.
- **Feed:** everything that happened to you or in public, with resource changes, points and the reason.
- **Defend:** barricades (+2, used up when a raid hits) and defense cards committed from the **Cards** tab. Defense cards are secret until a raid hits you.
- **Trade:** send an offer in the **Trade** tab. The other player accepts or rejects. Trades are final. Promises are not enforced.

## How a raid is decided
Your **attack** is 2 + mercenaries + card bonuses. The target's **defense** is their fort + barricade + other defenses. **You must beat defense, ties lose.** A failed raid costs you 2 gold. A successful one steals up to 6 resources and damages the fort.

## Tips
- Contested sites pay more (5 per trip against 3), but another clan can ambush them. Safe sites are a steady income.
- Keep your fort healthy: a fort at 0 is *exposed* and easy to raid, and destroying it is worth +3 points to the raider.
- A cheap barricade (3 materials) often forces an attacker to spend a mercenary or card to get through.
- Scouting costs 2 gold. Use it before spending your whole turn on a raid you can't win.
- Watch the 🔮 banner: events are announced a round ahead, so you can prepare (for example, don't hold more than 15 gold when the Goblin Tax is coming).
- Don't leave all your gold lying around. Spend it or park some with **Emergency Evacuation** before big raids.
- Remember upkeep: 1 food at the end of each round.

## If something goes wrong
- **Phone locked or Wi-Fi dropped?** Open the page again; you automatically return to your clan. The match never waits for you, but your submitted raid still happens. After 60 seconds offline your clan is marked inactive, and it comes back to life when you reconnect.
- **Eliminated?** You can keep watching, chatting and reading intel until the match ends.
